▚ ABOUT

404: boring mining not found.

MINESTOCK isn't another block game. It's a stock exchange wearing a Minecraft skin — every ore vein in the world is its own live market. The rarer the ore, the pricier the stock — and every dig moves the price.

Dig ore and the supply floods — the price dumps. Buy from the terminal and the demand pumps it. Random shocks shake the whole board every few minutes. The smart ones buy the dip without leaving the cave.

No install, no launcher, no team bag. Just a browser tab, a wallet as your save file, and a market that never sleeps because the miners never do.
